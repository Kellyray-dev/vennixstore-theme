# ISSUES FOUND

## Critical severity

1. **Catalog-wide missing image alt text:** all 107 public product records have at least one product image with a blank `alt` value in the public product JSON. This is an accessibility and SEO issue. Add descriptive, product-specific alt text for every product image.
2. **Catalog-wide unnormalized product types:** 70 products have blank product types and 20 use numeric product types (`0`, `4`, or `5`). Automated taxonomy collections cannot reliably operate until these values are replaced with approved product types.

## High severity

3. **Non-normalized legacy product types:** products 83, 84, 89, 90, 95, 96, 101, 103, 104, 105, and 107 use values such as `Women's Clothing`, `Fitness Accessories`, `Men's Underwear`, `Men's Jeans`, `Men Jeans`, `men`, `Men's Knitwear`, `mens wear`, and similar values outside the approved taxonomy.
4. **Wrong/unsafe feature classification risk:** products with blank or legacy types cannot reliably trigger the documented apparel detection rules. Product 84 is active equipment/accessory and should not be treated as ordinary apparel; product 106 is non-apparel and must never show clothing sizing.
5. **Wrong sizing data risk:** the public variant data for men's outerwear products 35, 36, and 57 uses clothing sizes but the compact audit found no clear size-token pattern in their exposed variant titles. Confirm variant option names in Admin and ensure their size data is intentional; this is a data-verification issue, not evidence that the products should be removed.

## Medium severity

6. **Missing material/composition data in product descriptions:** products 66, 70, 77, 84, 85, and 106 did not expose material/composition keywords in their public descriptions. Product 106 is non-apparel, so relevant specifications—not clothing materials—are required.
7. **Missing care data:** products 63–82 except products whose descriptions explicitly contain care text, plus 83–86, 87–88, 89, 91–93, 98–102, 106, and 107 did not expose care-instruction keywords in public descriptions. Apparel products need care instructions; non-apparel products need relevant maintenance/specification data instead.
8. **Tag normalization:** many products use supplier tags such as `Rhythmia`, `Ship From Overseas`, `fashion`, `featured`, `men`, and long descriptive tags rather than the normalized merchandising tags. Replace or supplement them with the approved lowercase tags; do not infer `best-seller` or `new-arrival` from supplier data.
9. **Duplicate product names:** “Men's Plus Size Zip Up Long Sleeve Jacket” appears at products 47 and 49 with different supplier tags. Confirm whether these are distinct products/SKUs or duplicates before merchandising.
